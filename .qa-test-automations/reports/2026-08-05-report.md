# QA Report — UI Readability and Visual Priority

## Outcome

- Result: PASS
- Automated checks: 55/55 passed (51 unit, 1 production build, 2 deterministic E2E, 1 hands-on play)
- Target: localhost only (`http://localhost:5178`), target guard passed

## Covered behavior

- Region, crisis, reward, achievement, milestone, and operation messages share one priority queue; at most one central announcement is rendered.
- Operation meetings and fever recaps suppress announcements while visible, then resume queued messages without visual overlap.
- Duplicate system float messages for region, operation, achievement, and quest completion are removed while tap gains remain immediate and spread across four side lanes.
- Related HUD and panel labels are at least 10px and body copy is at least 11px; the settings and close targets are 44px.
- The HUD no longer repeats CPS already shown in the world, while the fever summary is split into a readable title and hint.
- Stage, communism, goal, and event cooldown progress use `scaleX`; reduced-motion removes the relevant transitions.
- Mobile 390×844, compact 360×740, and desktop layouts have no horizontal overflow.

## Hands-on alpha result

The fresh-save 90-second run used only visible UI interactions and no GameState value injection. It reached area 16 with 734 taps, 69 upgrades, and 2 events. Completing 15 areas produced exactly 5 operation meetings, while 2 fever starts produced 2 finishers and 2 recovery phases. No page, console, or local network errors occurred.

Visual review caught and fixed two issues after the first implementation pass: a fever recap inherited a banner border behind it, and system completion floats duplicated queued central announcements. The final operation, recap, alert-priority, and compact-viewport screenshots show one central message path.
