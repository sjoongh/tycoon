## Covered behavior

- 12 operations are split evenly across tap, build, and response; each deterministic draft offers one per family.
- Operation meetings occur only after areas 3, 6, 9… and pending legacy drafts remain valid.
- The dominant family controls a 12-second fever: tap (tap ×3/production ×2), build (production ×5/25% temporary discount), response (production ×3/response reward ×2 and refreshed actions).
- The rush action appears only in the final four seconds, can be used once, and doubles the remaining fever effect.
- Fever ends in a non-blocking recap and a 30-second no-charge recovery.
- Mobile and desktop have no horizontal overflow; draft focus, 44px touch targets, keyboard focus style, and reduced-motion behavior pass.

## Hands-on alpha result

Completing 27 areas produced exactly 9 operation meetings, while 4 fever starts produced 4 finishers and 4 recovery phases. Additional 120-second family-locked runs reached area 21 with six build operations and area 18 with five response operations; both produced two fever cycles, two finishers, two recovery phases, and zero errors. The profiles differ clearly without either becoming blocked or continuously fevered.
