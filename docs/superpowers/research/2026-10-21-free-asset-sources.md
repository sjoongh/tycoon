## 5. Licensing hygiene

- Keep a `CREDITS` file with the CC BY lines: Antea, Chris Perich, Solar Granulation, extradave, game-icons.net authors (lorc, delapouite, skoll, badges, john-redman).
- Screenshot the informal "use it for whatever" pages (arlantr, AdricCustoms) at download time.
- Do not ship anything from Scenario free, Rosebud free/Indie, LimeZu free Modern Interiors, or ToffeeCraft free tiers.
- Unverified items to re-check before relying on them: PixelLab free-tier numbers, Retro Diffusion pricing, Leonardo free-tier terms, Lospec no-credit policy, Pixelkiln license text.
